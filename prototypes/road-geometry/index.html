What beats what

When a sign and a rule disagree, this order decides. The higher one always wins.

How the order works

  1. A traffic controller’s signal

    A police officer or traffic controller overrides lights, signs and rules.

    RVV art. 84

  2. Traffic lights

    A working light overrides the priority signs at the same junction.

    RVV art. 64

  3. Signs and road markings

    Give-way signs and haaientanden override the general rules.

    RVV art. 63

  4. General rules

    Traffic from the right, turning, trams and special manoeuvres such as leaving an exit.

    RVV chapter II
