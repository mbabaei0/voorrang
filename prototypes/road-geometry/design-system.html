Design system

Built from Dutch road furniture. A colour means here what its sign family means on the road.

Principles

  • Colour carries meaning. Yellow is priority and action, blue is guidance, red is a conflict. Never use one of them for decoration.
  • One bold thing per screen. On the landing page it is the playable crossroads. Everything around it stays quiet.
  • Structure only where the content has it. Numbers appear only for a real order: a passing order, the precedence list, the suggested topic order.
  • Plain words. Name things the way a learner driver would. Keep Dutch legal terms where the exam uses them, and explain them.

Type

One family, Archivo. Width does the work: headlines use its narrowest cut, like letters painted on the road; text stays at normal width. Sizes follow Bringhurst’s scale.
